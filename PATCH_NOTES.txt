Replacement timeslot suggestions

Adds a read-only option layer on top of the existing replacement engine.

Confirmed policy preserved:
1. lower operational load ranks first;
2. exact requested time is preferred among otherwise comparable providers;
3. a provider busy at that time is NOT excluded if another common patient/provider slot exists.

For one selected provider, free alternative slots are ordered by proximity to the requested time.
No workbook write-back is performed by show_replacement_options.py.
