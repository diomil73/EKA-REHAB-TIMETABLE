PATIENT-CENTRIC VISUAL PREVIEW

Purpose:
- Rebuild multi-assignment THERAPIST_DAILY cells from patient-owned assignments.
- Display same patient once when they have split day/treatment assignments.
- Show day-pattern information when multiple different patients share a cell.
- Date-label temporary replacement occurrences at the destination.
- Safety-stop when the selected destination time is actually occupied on that date.

This is a preview experiment. It does not change PATIENT_PLANNER or the baseline workbook.
