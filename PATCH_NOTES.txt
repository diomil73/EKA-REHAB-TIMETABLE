Adds read-only weekly validation for permanent patient assignments.

Use:
  python Python/tools/check_permanent_assignment.py --patient "ΣΑΚΕΡΛΗΣ" --to-provider "Πέτσιος" --to-time 13:00

The tool checks every weekday in the patient's recurring pattern against the 6-slot physiotherapist / 5-slot student hard limits and also reports same-day destination conflicts.
