REHAB replacement priority correction

This patch supersedes the previous student_management patch, which should NOT be applied separately.

Confirmed ranking:
1) lower operational daily load
2) exact requested/original time when load is equal
3) otherwise another common free timeslot keeps the provider eligible

Students:
- separate role from therapists
- may be replacement providers when active and replacement-capable
- maximum 5 daily patient/timeslots
- 4/5 can receive one more replacement
- 5/5 cannot receive a sixth slot

No Excel workbook is modified by this patch.
